Privacy Policy

Conference Registration & QR Pass App · Last updated: 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ved TRUST Foundation (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the [APP NAME] mobile application (the “App”). The App supports conference registration, email OTP verification, attendee photo submission, category-based digital passes, QR-code entry, and applicable food coupons.

1. Information we collect

  • Registration details: Your name, email address, and mobile number.
  • Verification information: One-time passwords (OTPs) and related email verification status and timestamps.
  • Photo: An attendee photo that you capture or submit for your conference profile or digital pass.
  • Conference information: Your registration or attendee ID, event details, and category (participant, speaker, or volunteer), including eligibility information for facilities such as food coupons.
  • Pass and usage records: Your unique QR-pass identifier and, where enabled, check-in and coupon redemption status.
  • Technical information: Basic security and operational logs needed to run and protect the App, if collected by our servers or service providers.

We do not require precise location, Bluetooth scanning, microphone recordings, or facial recognition for the features described above. If these features are added later, we will update this policy and relevant permission disclosures.

2. Permissions

  • Camera: Used when you choose to take your attendee photo. The App requests camera access only when needed for this feature.

If the App later supports selecting existing images, it may use the device's system photo picker. Actual permissions depend on the final App implementation.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and verify your conference registration using email OTP.
  • To link your photo and attendee category to your profile.
  • To generate and validate your unique QR code for event access.
  • To show eligible food coupons or other event-related benefits and prevent duplicate redemption.
  • To help authorized event administrators manage attendees, categories, check-ins, and support requests.
  • To maintain App security, prevent misuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing of information

Authorized conference administrators and event operations staff may access the details needed to organize and manage the event. We may also use service providers for secure hosting, database storage, file storage, and OTP email delivery. These providers should process information only for the services they perform. We may disclose information where legally required.

Hosting / email service providers used: Google Firebase (database and storage).

5. Data security

We aim to protect attendee data through HTTPS connections, access controls, and secure server-side validation of QR codes. Your QR code may be restricted from ordinary in-app screenshots where supported, but no mobile application can fully prevent a person from photographing a screen using another device. Never share your QR pass with others.

Encryption and security controls must be verified against the production implementation before publishing this policy.

6. Data retention

We keep registration, photo, QR-pass, and event-usage data only for as long as necessary for conference operations, support, security, or legal obligations. The actual retention periods are: attendee photos are deleted after 180 days; registration details, QR-pass and event-usage records are kept for 1 year after the conference ends. After that, information is deleted or anonymized according to our retention procedure.

7. Account and data deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting vtrustf@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject “Conference App – Data Deletion Request”.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for authorized conference attendees. If children are permitted to register, appropriate age-based notices, consent, and data-handling measures must be implemented as required by applicable law and Google Play policies. The App is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App's features or data practices change. We will post the updated policy here with a revised “Last updated” date.
